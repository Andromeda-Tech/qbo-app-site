Andromeda Books Agent

Andromeda Books Agent is an internal software tool built and operated by Andromeda Tech, LLC. It connects to Andromeda Tech's own QuickBooks Online company to help its managing partners keep the books: reconciling bank activity, recording subcontractor payments against the right contracts, and preparing reports.

The tool is not offered to the public or to other QuickBooks users. It is used only by authorized members of Andromeda Tech, LLC.

Connecting to QuickBooks is done by an authorized member from Andromeda Tech's own computer. There is nothing to sign up for on this site.
